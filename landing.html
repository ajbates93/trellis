The problem

Feedback gets lost. Growth stalls. Reviews become guesswork.

Most teams rely on annual reviews and scattered Slack messages. Feedback is inconsistent, managers lack visibility, and employees don't know where they stand until it's too late.

The solution

Everything your team needs to grow

Weekly Peer Feedback

Lightweight weekly check-ins make feedback a habit, not an event. One review per person per week keeps things consistent and low-pressure.

Buddy System

Pair everyone with a buddy who reviews, publishes, and supports their growth. Buddies see all feedback and help turn it into action.

Goal Tracking

Set personal development goals with milestones and deadlines. Track progress over time and connect goals directly to the feedback you receive.

🌱

Trellis Garden

A living visualisation of your team's feedback health. Watch your garden grow from wilting to thriving as your team builds the feedback habit.

Insights & Analytics

Sentiment analysis, engagement trends, and contribution leaderboards. Admins get a clear picture of team health at a glance.

Easy Team Setup

Import your team via CSV, assign buddies, and go. Invitation-based access means your people see their relationships from the moment they log in.

How it works

Up and running in three steps

1

Set up your team

Import your team with a CSV or add people manually. Assign buddy pairs and roles. Everyone gets an invite to log in with SSO.

2

Give feedback weekly

Each week, team members share a short piece of feedback for a colleague. Submit directly in the app or via the Slack integration. It takes under two minutes.

3

Grow together

Buddies review and publish feedback. Team members set goals, track progress, and walk into reviews with a full record of their growth.

Available now

Slack Integration

Connect Trellis to your Slack workspace as an optional integration. Once approved by a workspace admin, your team can use the /submit slash command to give feedback without leaving Slack.

Authentication

Single sign-on

No new passwords to manage. Your team logs in with Google today, with more SSO providers coming soon including Microsoft and SAML. Invitation-based access keeps things secure, so only people you've added can get in.
